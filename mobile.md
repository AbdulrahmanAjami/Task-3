iPhone 15 Pro Review
Rating: 9/10

Apple's premium flagship introduces subtle design refinements and massive performance gains. Here is a quick look at the key features:

Design & Build: The shift from stainless steel to a brushed Titanium frame makes the phone significantly lighter in hand while improving durability. The new customizable Action Button replaces the old mute switch, adding great utility.

Performance: Powered by the industry-first A17 Pro chip, this device handles heavy multitasking and console-quality gaming with absolute ease, while maintaining impressive power efficiency.

Camera System: The 48MP main camera captures stunningly detailed photos with richer colors. Portrait mode is now automatic, allowing you to adjust depth and focus after taking the shot.

Conclusion:
With the long-awaited addition of the universal USB-C port and a lighter titanium body, the iPhone 15 Pro is a highly polished, powerful device that sets a new benchmark for flagship smartphones.
